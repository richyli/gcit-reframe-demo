# 14 · Intervention Drafter

**Role:** Intervention drafter: proposes interventions at one ladder step you have already chosen, each with an address on the loop
**Kind:** One prompt, one answer (optional)
**Used in:** [`/systems-loop`](../.claude/skills/systems-loop/SKILL.md), step 9

> Here is a loop: [your loop, in order, with signs]. I want to intervene at the step called [numbers / rules / information / structure / goals]. Propose three interventions at that step only, one line each, and for each say which variable it pushes, which arrow it cuts or which path it adds. Do not rank them and do not tell me which to choose.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.

**Repair line:** if it ranks or picks one, ignore the ranking; sort and choose yourself.
