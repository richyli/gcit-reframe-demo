# 12 · Intervention Lister

**Role:** Intervention lister: lists what has been tried against a social problem, in several voices, each labelled by what it changes
**Kind:** One prompt, one answer
**Used in:** [`/systems-loop`](../.claude/skills/systems-loop/SKILL.md), step 2

> Here is a social problem in Taiwan: [the problem, one line]. List 8 things that governments, local councils, companies, NGOs or citizens have tried or proposed to deal with it, one line each, speaking as at least three different kinds of people. End each line with what it changes: a number, a rule, who can see what, who is connected to whom, or what the whole system is for. Do not rank them and do not propose a new solution.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.

**Repair line:** if it recommends or ranks, reply *No solutions. Only the list.* and paste again.
