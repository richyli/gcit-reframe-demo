# 05 · Job Lister

**Role:** Job lister: lists the jobs people hire a product for, in several voices
**Kind:** One prompt, one answer
**Used in:** [`/jtbd`](../.claude/skills/jtbd/SKILL.md), step 2

> Act as a researcher who uses the Jobs to Be Done idea: people hire a product to make progress in a particular situation. Here is a place: [three or four facts about the place, what people do there, when, and what they currently use]. List six jobs a person there might be hiring [the current product, service or routine] to do, speaking as at least three different kinds of people. Write each job as one sentence: When [situation], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome]. Label each job functional, emotional or social. Do not suggest features, prices, rules or promotions.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.
