# 06 · Six-Whys Outsider

**Role:** Outsider on your own place: asks why, six times, one at a time
**Kind:** Multi-turn: one question at a time
**Used in:** [`/hidden-assumption`](../.claude/skills/hidden-assumption/SKILL.md), step 2

> I want to find a hidden assumption in a place I know well. The place and my role: [one line, for example: I work at the front desk of a cram school]. The problem people there talk about: [How can we ...?]. Play someone who has never been inside a place like this and does not know how it usually works. Ask me six questions, one at a time, and wait for my answer before asking the next. Each question must pick up something I just said and ask why it has to be that way, or what would happen if we stopped doing it. After my sixth answer, list every sentence of mine that amounts to "this is just how it is", quoting my own words, one per line. Do not suggest solutions, do not use the special words of my field, and do not tell me which assumption matters most.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.
