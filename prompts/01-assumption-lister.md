# 01 · Assumption Lister

**Role:** Lister: lists the assumptions built into a problem
**Kind:** One prompt, one answer
**Used in:** [`/assumption-challenge`](../.claude/skills/assumption-challenge/SKILL.md), step 2

> Here is a problem [who wrote it, for example: a university club president] wrote: "[the old frame]". List 8 assumptions built into this question, meaning things that must be true for this to be the right question to ask. Write each one as a short sentence starting with "It assumes". Do not suggest any solutions and do not rank the assumptions.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.
