# 08 · Strongest Objector

**Role:** Objector: gives the strongest objection and the trade-off
**Kind:** One prompt, one answer
**Used in:** [`/triz`](../.claude/skills/triz/SKILL.md), step 2

> Here is a problem: [your redefined problem]. The first idea is: [your idea]. Play the person in this place who would object most strongly, and give the strongest objection in one or two sentences with a concrete reason taken from the situation. Then rewrite it as "If we improve [A], then [B] gets worse." Do not suggest solutions.

Replace every `[ ]` with your own content before sending.
